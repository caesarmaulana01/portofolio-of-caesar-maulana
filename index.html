DrivEye - Intelligent Vehicle Distance Measurement
  • Developed "DrivEye", a mobile application that leverages the YOLOv5 model for real-time vehicle distance measurement.
  • Enhanced road safety by providing early warnings about safe distances between vehicles.
  • Achieved a mean Average Precision (mAP) of 90% in object detection.
  • Attained a distance prediction accuracy of 94.08%.
  • Offers a practical and cost-effective solution to reduce traffic accidents and promote safer driving behaviors.
  • Published in the IEEE Xplore Digital Library.
